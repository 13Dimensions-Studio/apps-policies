Account Deletion Requests

Follow the steps below to request deletion of your account and associated data for any of our apps.

Lunaris

Wallpaper & Media  ·  Firebase Account

Step 1 — Send deletion email
13dimensions.studio@gmail.com
Subject: Lunaris account and data deletion

Email from the address registered to your Lunaris account. In the message, state that you want your account and associated data deleted. Do not include your password.

✓ Will be deleted

  • Firebase Authentication account
  • Profile data (name, email, timestamps)
  • Synchronized favorite wallpaper IDs
  • Base download-quota records

⚠ Not automatically deleted

  • Wallpaper exports on your device
  • Google Play subscription (cancel separately)
  • Records kept by service providers for legal/security reasons
📱

You can also start a request from inside Lunaris by opening Profile → Contact Us.

General Information

Processing Timeline & Questions

We aim to process verified account deletion requests within 30 days of receipt. Only apps with user accounts are listed on this page. Apps without accounts (Slither Saga, Tic-Tac-Toe, Space Invaders, SafePing, Ledgerly, Momentum) store data only on your device — uninstall the app or clear its storage to remove local data.

For any questions, email 13dimensions.studio@gmail.com.
